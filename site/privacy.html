Privacy Policy

Your information, handled with care.

Last updated: November 25, 2026

What we collect

If you fill in the consultation form on our Contact page, we receive the details you type: your name, company, email address, phone number, the topic you need help with, and any message you add. We do not collect anything else about you through this website.

How we use it

We use these details only to reply to your request and to follow up about the services you asked about. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who processes the form

The form is delivered to us by email through FormSubmit, a third-party form service. Your submission passes through that service on its way to our inbox. Please do not include sensitive information, such as banking details or government ID numbers, in the form.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising or analytics trackers. Your light or dark mode choice is saved in your own browser so the site remembers it on your next visit. It is not sent to us.

Keeping and removing your information

We keep your request for as long as we need it to answer you and to support the work we do together. You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete what we hold about you.
